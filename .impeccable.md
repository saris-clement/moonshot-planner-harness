## Design Context

### Users

The primary user is the planner researcher running local, agent-driven Phase 2 experiments. They need to monitor concurrent variants, inspect requirement-level evidence, review LLM suggestions, record human truth, and decide which change should become the next parent.

### Brand Personality

Evidence-first, rigorous, operational. The interface should feel like a purpose-built engineering instrument rather than a promotional website or generic SaaS dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

A Langfuse-inspired industrial research console: a system sans stack, white and cool-gray surfaces, navy primary controls, muted lavender links, hairline gray rules, 8px radii, and compact 32px controls. Monospace is reserved for identifiers and measurements. It is light-only and avoids oversized marketing typography, neon-on-dark AI styling, generic card grids, glass effects, and decorative dashboards.

### Design Principles

1. Separate measured facts, LLM suggestions, and human-verified truth visually.
2. Make experiment lineage and frozen inputs impossible to overlook.
3. Favor dense, scannable comparisons over decorative summaries.
4. Preserve every critical action on narrow screens rather than hiding controls.
5. Use motion only to communicate live state changes and respect reduced motion.
